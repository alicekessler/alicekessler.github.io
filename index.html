About Me

I went to Iowa State University for my undergraduate as well as my master's degree in mathematics, which I completed under Dr. Shlomo Gelaki on Tensor Categories. Currently I am a PhD student at The Ohio State University under Dr. Sanjeevi Krishnan. My interests are in categorical homotopy theory and homotopy type theory.
